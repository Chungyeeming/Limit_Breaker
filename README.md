For over a decade, Minecraft players have been artificially confined to a 30,000,000 block radius. 
This was never the true edge of the world—it was a hardcoded safety measure designed to hide the mathematical breakdown of the game's engine. 
Limit Breaker completely shatters this artificial barrier, 
unleashing the underlying rendering and world-generation systems to reach the absolute 32-bit integer limit of Minecraft: 
`X/Z: 2,147,483,647`.

However, 
to reach the astronomical figure of 2.1 billion is not just as simple as removing the borders of the world, 
you will encounter all kinds of crashes as well as errors when you exceed 30 million grids. 
However, this mod miraculously fixes these bugs, 
allowing players to continue exploring without encountering any bugs and crashes.

Limit Breaker updates very quickly every time a new version of Minecraft is released.

We didn't just remove the world borders; **WE REWROTE THE MATHEMATICAL LOGIC OF MINECRAFT!**

***THIS MOD IS STILL IN EXPERIMENT! IT'S VERY UNSTABLE; DON'T USE IT ON YOUR VERY IMPORTANT MAPS***

## Core Features and  Fixes

* **World Border Expansion:** Expands the world border from a mere 30 million to the absolute physical maximum of the 32-bit integer limit.


* **Fixes of extreme coordinates:** Fixed lighting system crash, entity subset overflow, VoxelShape collideX not working, and the code that caused crashes / error due to human intervention has been removed.


* **Fixed rendering:** Fixed the issue of not rendering at 2.1 billion blocks.

## Known incompatible mods
* **Sodium** (There might be endlessly repeating rocky terrain outside the boundaries; while it doesn't affect the experience, it's ugly.)
* **C2ME** (Crash)
* **Lithium** (Still crash lol)


In short, any mods that change the render settings might cause instability. The author will try to fix it, but if they can't, I don't know what to do, haha.
